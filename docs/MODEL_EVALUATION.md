## 1. Executive Summary

This report documents the baseline quantitative evaluation of the **Dual-Stream Deepfake Detector** (`ConvNeXt-Small` + `BayarConv2d` / `FFT` frequency residual stream). The model is designed to detect synthetic facial manipulation across still images and video frames.

To adhere to strict scientific rigor (**NFR-01, NFR-02, NFR-03, NFR-04**), all evaluation experiments are conducted on **fixed, identity-disjoint test manifests** with deterministic seeds. No test samples overlap in speaker identity with training or validation splits.

---

## 2. Model Architecture & Calibration Specifications

* **Spatial Branch:** `ConvNeXt-Small` pretrained on ImageNet-1k, extracting high-level semantic anomalies, facial boundary blending seams, and eye/lip alignment defects.
* **Frequency & Noise Stream:** `BayarConv2d` (constrained adaptive spatial rich model filters) combined with 2D Discrete Fourier Transform (FFT) log-magnitude spectrums to capture high-frequency upsampling grid noise generated by GAN and Diffusion architectures.
* **Temperature Calibration:** Logits are calibrated using Temperature Scaling ($T = 1.4788347$) to align predicted confidence probabilities with empirical accuracy.
* **Operational Decision Threshold:** $\tau = 0.01$ (optimised for forensic screening where false negatives carry higher risk).

## 7. Model Limitations & Failure Modes

1. **Heavy Compression Vulnerability:** Heavy JPEG recompression ($Q \le 50$) suppresses high-frequency noise residuals in the BayarConv2d/FFT stream, reducing ROC-AUC by over 13%.
2. **Out-of-Distribution (OOD) Domain Shift:** When evaluated on Celeb-DF v2 (which uses advanced color restoration and blending), the false positive rate increases from 12.8% to 29.2%.
3. **No-Face / Multi-Face Media:** The face-focused cropper requires at least one detectable face. Media lacking clear facial regions skips inference with a structured warning.
4. **Diffusion / Generative AI Artifact Shift:** Models trained strictly on GAN-based face swaps exhibit reduced sensitivity to modern Latent Diffusion generated portraits.
